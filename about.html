About Us

Welcome to FreeDocTools, a free online website providing simple tools for working with PDF files and images.

Our goal is to make common file-related tasks easier and more convenient. Our tools are designed to be simple to use without requiring complicated software.

Our Tools

FreeDocTools currently provides tools such as:

  • JPG to PDF
  • Image Compressor
  • Image Resizer
  • Merge PDF
  • Compress PDF

We are continuously working on improving the website and adding useful features.
